---
title: He Keeps Me Singing
summary: A reminder that God’s calling is stronger than our discouragement and His message cannot be kept silent.
scripture: Jeremiah 20:9
order: 1
---

Some songs are born out of celebration. Others come through disappointment, unanswered prayers, and the quiet moments when God whispers, “Keep going.”

After receiving a deeply discouraging email about one of my songs, I seriously considered laying songwriting aside. Yet instead of silence, God began filling my heart with new songs. Everywhere I looked, He seemed to place another melody, another lyric, another reminder that His calling had not changed.

That is how **He Keeps Me Singing** was born. Inspired by Jeremiah 20:9, it is a reminder that when God places His message within us, discouragement does not get the final word.
