This song was written during one of life’s storms. In the middle of that season, God placed a simple reminder on my heart:

> “Keep going, Billy. You’re working on your well done.”

**Working On A Well Done** is for anyone who needs strength to take the next faithful step. The road may be hard and the answers may not come quickly, but God’s faithfulness remains.
